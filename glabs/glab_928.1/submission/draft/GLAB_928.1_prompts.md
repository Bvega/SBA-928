# GLAB 928.1 Prompt Set

## Task 1 Original Prompt

Generate a comprehensive report which analyzes customer feedback.

## Task 1 Analysis Prompt for Screenshot

Evaluate the following business prompt based on its specificity, context, and desired output format. Identify its strengths and weaknesses, explain what important details are missing, and recommend improvements that would help an AI generate a useful and actionable business report.

Original prompt: "Generate a comprehensive report which analyzes customer feedback."

## Task 2 Specificity Prompt

Generate a comprehensive report analyzing customer feedback collected from January through June 2026 about our retail mobile application's user interface and checkout process. Examine app-store reviews, post-purchase surveys, and customer-support tickets. Identify recurring usability problems, checkout failures, payment concerns, navigation issues, and positive feedback. Quantify recurring themes when the source data permits, distinguish isolated comments from repeated patterns, and identify the three issues with the greatest likely effect on conversion and customer satisfaction.

## Task 3 Context Prompt

Generate a comprehensive report analyzing customer feedback collected from January through June 2026 about our retail mobile application's user interface and checkout process. Examine app-store reviews, post-purchase surveys, and customer-support tickets. The product and customer-experience teams will use the report to prioritize the next quarterly development roadmap, reduce checkout abandonment, and improve customer satisfaction. Identify recurring usability problems, checkout failures, payment concerns, navigation issues, and positive feedback. Quantify recurring themes when the source data permits, distinguish isolated comments from repeated patterns, rank the three highest-priority issues by business impact and urgency, and recommend practical actions for product, engineering, and customer-support leaders.

## Task 4 Final Enhanced Prompt

Act as a senior customer-insights analyst. Create a management-ready report analyzing customer feedback collected from January through June 2026 about our retail mobile application's user interface and checkout process. Use app-store reviews, post-purchase surveys, and customer-support tickets as the source categories. The product and customer-experience teams will use the report to prioritize the next quarterly development roadmap, reduce checkout abandonment, and improve customer satisfaction.

Structure the report with these sections: Executive Summary; Data Scope and Limitations; Key Findings; Theme Analysis; Positive Feedback; Priority Matrix; Recommendations; and Next Steps. For each major theme, state the supporting evidence, frequency or percentage when available, affected customer journey stage, likely business impact, and confidence level. Separate recurring patterns from isolated comments. Rank the three highest-priority issues by impact and urgency. Provide specific recommendations with an owner, expected benefit, suggested timeline, and measurable success indicator. Include a table summarizing the major themes and recommend appropriate bar or trend charts without inventing data. Clearly label assumptions, gaps, and any conclusion that the available feedback does not support. Use concise professional language suitable for presentation to senior management.
